The ALL lab is officially launching! 🚀 

We are recruiting a junior researcher ("incarico di ricerca" research position) to work on the OPEN BAR project (see the [OPEN BAR project page]({{ '/projects/openbar/' | relative_url }}) for more details). The purpose of the position is to address the replicability issues for studies reporting an advantage in attentional control efficiency for people who speak two or more languages compared to those who speak only one. Specifically, the researcher will contribute to the development of a web app to facilitate the direct replication of bilingual-advantage studies and will carry out direct replication for a sample of them. 

The position is for 3 years with a gross annual salary of €22,500.00. Applicants must hold a Master’s degree in Psychology, Linguistics, or related disciplines, obtained no longer than 6 years before the call's deadline, and be competent in cognitive psychology, cognitive neuroscience, and/or psychometrics. The ideal candidate has some experience in systematic literature reviews, including the extraction and structured annotation of data, materials, and/or code from scientific publications; basic knowledge of sample planning, designing and conducting behavioral experiments, and data analysis; familiarity with the principles and practices of open science, particularly pre-registration and transparent management of data, materials, and/or code; and strong communication skills in English.
