**Principal Investigator**

My main interest is in adaptive control, people's ability to adapt cognitive control based on their goal and the situation they are in. I'm also interested in visual word recognition and spoken word production in monolingual and multilingual individuals. Bridging these two interests is the "bilingual advantage hypothesis", the question of whether speaking more than one language results in more efficient attentional control. My research aims to shed light on these issues through the analysis of (mainly, behavioral) experimental and cross-sectional data from the healthy adult population.
